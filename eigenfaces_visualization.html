Eigenvalue Spectrum

Distribution of eigenvalues across all 361 principal components

Spectrum Interpretation

  • The spectrum shows that the largest eigenvalues occur in the early components (left side)
  • This indicates that the first few principal components capture most of the variance
  • The long tail of small eigenvalues means later components contribute less to explaining facial variation

Cumulative Variance Explained

Cumulative variance explained as more principal components are included

Variance Analysis

  • The red dashed line marks 90% cumulative variance
  • Need ~287 components (out of 361) to explain 90% of variance
  • This shows that face images have significant redundancy
  • Dimensionality reduction from 361 → 287 dims saves ~20% of space while retaining 90% information
  • Further reduction to ~150 components might retain ~70-80% variance

Complete Eigenfaces Grid

4×5 grid of the first 20 principal component eigenfaces

Key Observations

  • Early eigenfaces (0-3) show broad lighting and overall shape variations
  • Middle eigenfaces (4-10) capture medium-frequency facial features
  • Later eigenfaces (11+) capture finer details and textures
  • Each eigenface is orthogonal - captures independent variation pattern
  • Random data → eigenfaces look like noise patterns (as expected)
